Start Here

New to Orthodox history? This page walks through it in order. You never need to read it all at once.

  1. Scroll down through time

    The timeline runs from the oldest books of the Old Testament at the top to the present at the bottom. Large gold banners mark each era, and small labels mark each century.

  2. Tap anything to learn more

    Every entry opens a card with a plain-language explanation and links to read the original texts free online. Use the arrows in the card to step to the next entry.

  3. Narrow it down

    Use the buttons in the bar to show only one kind of entry, such as only the councils, or jump straight to an era or a century.

  4. Follow a study path

    Not sure where to begin? Open and pick a guided walk through one topic, one step at a time. Paths marked "Start here" are made for newcomers.

  5. Search by name

    Type a name like "Chrysostom", "Nicaea" or "Tobit" in the search box to find it instantly.

About the dates. Bible books are placed by when they were most likely written, not by the events they describe. For many Old Testament books the Church's tradition and modern scholars disagree, and both dates are given. "c." means "about."

About the perspective. This timeline tells the story as the Orthodox Church understands it. Where Roman Catholics or Protestants see things differently, their view is stated fairly next to the Orthodox answer from Scripture, the Fathers and the councils.

About the links. Reading links point only to Orthodox sources, such as OrthodoxWiki. Where a reliable Orthodox copy of a text has not been found yet, the card says "Orthodox link needed".
